The Problem

No web presence, and litigation firms can't afford accessibility risk.

A newly formed California trust & estate litigation firm needed a professional web presence from scratch — one that could hold up to the scrutiny of prospective clients evaluating a law firm during an already stressful moment.

Law firm websites are also a common target for ADA/accessibility demand letters. The firm needed a site built correctly from day one, not retrofitted after a complaint.

The Solution

A fast, accessible marketing site plus a private staff portal.

Designed and built end-to-end: information architecture, practice area pages, attorney profiles, and a full accessibility pass against WCAG 2.2 AA success criteria — keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility, color contrast, and semantic markup.

  • • Six practice area pages (trust litigation, will contests, conservatorships, probate, estate planning, and more)
  • • Full WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility audit and remediation
  • • Private staff portal (Supabase-authenticated) separate from the public marketing site
  • • SEO foundation: Search Console verification, sitemap, structured metadata
  • • Static architecture for fast load times and minimal attack surface

Why It Matters

A template for law firm websites done right.

The Problem

Paper forms, manual entry, zero visibility.

R.F. MacDonald Co. is one of the largest boiler and burner service companies on the U.S. West Coast, with 12 offices across California and Nevada serving commercial, industrial, and institutional customers.

25 field salespeople were generating 100–125 quotes per month each. Every quote started on paper or a tablet, then got manually entered into NetSuite by inside sales.

That's 50–100 hours of data entry per salesperson per month. Errors, bottlenecks, and zero visibility for managers into what the field team was actually doing.

The Solution

A full production sales management application.

Not a prototype — a system used daily by a real company. Built from discovery call to production deployment by one person.

  • • 9 digitized sales forms with dedicated calculation engines, validated against real customer quote spreadsheets
  • • 31-table PostgreSQL database with Row-Level Security on every table
  • • Five-tier role-based approval workflow, from field sales up to executive admin
  • • File cabinet module: customer/job document tree, photo and CAD file uploads, public shareable job links
  • • PDF/Excel report exports plus direct NetSuite CSV export integration
  • • Executive dashboard: KPIs, trends, status distribution across all offices
  • • Mobile-first PWA, installable on phones for field use

Results

What changed.

Digitized paper-based sales workflow across 12 offices
Real-time manager visibility into field sales activity for the first time
Automated quote calculations that previously required manual spreadsheet work
Approval pipeline that eliminated bottleneck between field and office
Designed to save the company 1,000+ hours per month in manual data entry
Delivered beyond contract scope: labor rates admin, printable reports, CSV export, audit logging

The Problem

Court dockets change, and nobody finds out until it's late.

Small trust & estate firms track cases across spreadsheets and manual court-website checks. Hearing dates move, filings get added, judges get reassigned — and firms find out by chance, or too late.

Generic case management tools don't understand California probate court structure, and none of them watch the docket for you.

The Solution

Case management with a built-in docket-watching engine.

Docketed pairs a purpose-built cases and clients system with an automated change-detection engine that watches California Superior Court sources and flags what moved.

  • • Full case management: status transitions, parties, private notes, tasks, secure document storage
  • • Automated court monitoring engine: detects hearing date changes, new filings, and status or party changes
  • • Live monitoring sources for Orange County probate tentative rulings and LA Superior Court case calendars
  • • Email ingestion: firms forward court notification emails and case updates extract automatically
  • • Role-scoped dashboard: urgent cases, upcoming court dates, and alerts at a glance
  • • Multi-tenant architecture built for multiple firms from day one

Why It Matters

What's live today.

Foundation, cases module, and court monitoring engine shipped and in active beta use
Beta customer: Prudential Advocate, a California trust & estate litigation firm
Multi-tenant from the ground up, ready to onboard additional firms
Actively expanding court coverage across additional California counties

The Concept

Show, don't tell.

Most agencies show mockups. We built a fully working application that visitors can click through directly in the browser. No login, no signup — just open the demo and start exploring.

Rise & Proof is a fictional bakery with real operational complexity: wholesale orders, recipe costing, inventory tracking, and role-based access for different team members.

The demo runs entirely in the browser — no backend, no database, no stored data. Just the frontend patterns and calculation engines that power production applications.

What It Includes

Five modules, three roles, real logic.

  • • Dashboard — KPIs, revenue charts, sales breakdown, and recent orders at a glance
  • • Orders — Filterable order management with status tracking and assignee visibility
  • • Recipes — Live cost calculator with ingredient management, batch scaling, and profit margins
  • • Inventory — Stock levels, reorder points, and inventory valuation
  • • Role Switching — Owner sees everything, Kitchen sees production, Front of House sees their orders
  • • Mobile Responsive — Full mobile layout with bottom navigation and card-based views
